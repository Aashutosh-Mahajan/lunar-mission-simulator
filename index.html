Let go of the keys and the lander stops drifting and settles itself down. On launch the computer stages, steers and cuts off for you; in the coast it cuts the burns off on target — you only hold Space.

Pilot & Commander — you fly it

GDescent-rate hold — throttles for you (P66 mode)

Pilot kills your drift whenever you let go of the steering keys, stages the rocket automatically and gives wider margins. Commander is direct control at the real Apollo limits. Land a site on each setting to earn its bronze, silver and gold medals.

How to land it

Thrust comes out of the top of the lander, so leaning is how you steer — and S is the brake. Lean back with S while holding Space to kill forward drift; A and D push you left and right.

Attitude hold caps the lean at 45° and flies you back upright the moment you release the key, so you cannot flip over. The order that works: kill the drift first, high up, then get over the pad, then bring the descent rate under the gear limit. Arriving fast and sideways is what breaks the vehicle. The amber needle on the DRIFT dial is the direction you are sliding — lean the opposite way until it centres.

Both burns are graded on ΔV: hold the engine until the gauge is between the two gates, then cut off with I. Overburning is as bad as underburning. Time warp cancels itself as the band approaches, so you will not blow through it.

How to reach orbit

The pitch program flies the gravity turn — leave it alone through the thick air; a large angle of attack there breaks the vehicle. Above about 32 km a magenta FD bracket appears on the pitch tape: use W/S to hold the amber CMD needle inside it and the climb flattens into orbit by itself. Stage when a tank runs dry (automatic on Cadet and Pilot). Cut off with I when altitude, horizontal velocity and flight path angle are all inside the insertion window.
